# ENVIRONMENT

_Low-poly, modular, readable. Reference feel: PEAK, R.E.P.O. See `ART_DIRECTION.md`._

## Approach
- Modular kits: a few structural pieces + props recombined into many spaces.
- Simple recognizable shapes, strong silhouettes, clear gameplay spaces.
- Free assets first (Unity Asset Store free, or generated with Astra later), normalized to one look.
- Readability under chaos over visual density.
