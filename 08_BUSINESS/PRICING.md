The median of the co-op social cluster sits at or below $10. The outliers above $12 are either older, larger in scope, or single-player.

## What the conversion data says
Steam wishlist to first-week sales converts at roughly 10 to 15% on median, and games priced above $10 sit at the low end of that band. For the closest current comparable, analysts recommend $5.99 to $6.99 at launch with a possible increase after the first week, warning that pricing above $10 sharply slows sales in the price-sensitive regions that dominate this audience.

## Working assumption (not a decision)
Plan the product so that it is coherent at **$6 to $9**. That is a design constraint on scope as much as a price: at this price nobody expects a campaign, and a strong loop with thin content is acceptable.

## Do not
- Do not set a price before the product is understood.
- Do not use price as a differentiator. Low price is an entry ticket in this genre, not a position (see `../01_RESEARCH/WHITE_SPACES.md`).
- Do not model revenue from wishlists without applying the conversion factor.
