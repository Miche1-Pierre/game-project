# BUSINESS MODEL

_Simple and proven for this space._

## Model
- **Premium, one-time purchase** on Steam (PC). No free-to-play, no microtransactions, no ads.
- Price in the corpus band (roughly $8 to $15), decided with the concept (`PRICING.md`).
- Possible later: paid DLC or cosmetic content, only if the game succeeds and it fits.
- **50/50** between Pierre and Jonathan on costs and revenue (`TEAM_AGREEMENT.md`), pending final agreement.
- No company formed until revenue justifies it; taxes handled per the actual entity and countries (EU-based, selling worldwide).

## Why
The whole corpus succeeds on cheap premium co-op. It fits our cost model and needs no backend.
