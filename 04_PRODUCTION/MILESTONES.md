# MILESTONES

_Plan stated by the team at the 2026-09-16 concept meeting. Sequence is firm, durations are intentions, not commitments._

## M1. Greybox, about one week
The grandmother's house, scope frozen in `../02_GAME_DESIGN/GREYBOX_SPEC.md`.

**Exit condition, one question:** is it funny? Spontaneous laughter is mandatory. Without it the concept goes back to design and no assets are produced.

## M2. V1, about one week after M1
Conditional on M1 passing. The first map becomes a real playable level.

- The shop.
- The truck as an actual drivable vehicle, with physics and collisions.
- Whatever else the greybox showed was missing.
- Still one map.

The vigilance system, meaning behaviour-driven detection by the owner, is the V2 candidate and is explicitly not in V1.

## M3. One month of communication, running in parallel with production
Starts once V1 exists and the tutorial works end to end.

- Record video, publish the Steam page, open wishlists.
- Produce short-form video continuously.
- Start building a community.
- **Keep building the next maps during this month.** The month is not a pause.

Go-to-market detail is treated as its own workstream after the greybox passes: look at what worked and failed for comparable games, then maximise the wishlist. See `../10_MARKETING/WISHLIST_STRATEGY.md`.

## M4. Beta
At the end of the communication month.

External test protocol, decided at the meeting: 16 players in 4 teams of 4. One team including both developers, one team of friends left fully autonomous, two teams of strangers. The stranger teams are the signal. See `../11_TESTING/PLAYTESTS.md`.

## M5. Release
Not scheduled. Price, date and scope depend on the wishlist signal and the final look. See `../08_BUSINESS/PRICING.md`.

## Ordering rule
Unchanged from `../00_PROJECT/ROADMAP.md`: mechanic, interaction, consequence, loop, multiplayer, validation gate, content, art, audio, polish gate, launch. Never the reverse.

## Note on estimates
The one-week and two-week figures are the team's own targets. Earlier duration estimates produced by the agent were rejected as unreliable and have been removed rather than argued. Treat every duration here as a hypothesis to be measured against M1, which is the first real data point we will have.
