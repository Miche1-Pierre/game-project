# MILESTONES

_Concrete milestones, from OBJECTIVES and ROADMAP, updated with the plan the team stated at the 2026-09-16 concept meeting. Sequence is firm, durations are intentions, not commitments._

- **M0. Foundation (done):** research + decision repository.
- **M1. Concept + spec (done):** concept locked (ADR-003), `../02_GAME_DESIGN/GREYBOX_SPEC.md` written, Unity project created.

## M2. Greybox, about one week
**Partly done.** Tutorial_01 exists and runs in `UnityProject/`: grab, carry, truck loading, contract, HUD. Single player, primitives only.

Remaining for this milestone: play it, then decide. The greybox is what settles the open verb divergence recorded in `../02_GAME_DESIGN/GAME_CONCEPT.md`.

**Exit condition, one question:** is it fun and does it already generate stories? Spontaneous laughter is the criterion the team set. Without it the concept goes back to design and no assets are produced.

## M3. V1, about one week after M2
Conditional on M2 passing. The first map becomes a real playable level.

- Co-op, which is the immediate next step after the core feel is good.
- The shop.
- The truck as an actual drivable vehicle, with physics and collisions.
- Whatever the greybox showed was missing.
- Still one map.

The vigilance system, meaning behaviour-driven detection by an owner, is a V2 candidate and is explicitly not in V1.

## M4. One month of communication, running in parallel with production
Starts once V1 exists and the tutorial works end to end.

- Record video, publish the Steam page, open wishlists.
- Produce short-form video continuously.
- Start building a community.
- **Keep building the next maps during this month.** The month is not a pause.

Go-to-market detail is treated as its own workstream after the greybox passes: look at what worked and failed for comparable games, then maximise the wishlist. See `../10_MARKETING/WISHLIST_STRATEGY.md`.

## M5. Beta
At the end of the communication month.

External test protocol, decided at the meeting: 16 players in 4 teams of 4. One team including both developers, one team of friends left fully autonomous, two teams of strangers. The stranger teams are the signal. See `../11_TESTING/PLAYTESTS.md`.

## M6. Release
Not scheduled. Price, date and scope depend on the wishlist signal and the final look. See `../08_BUSINESS/PRICING.md`.

## Ordering rule
Unchanged from `../00_PROJECT/ROADMAP.md`: mechanic, interaction, consequence, loop, multiplayer, validation gate, content, art, audio, polish gate, launch. Never the reverse.

Each milestone has a clear exit test (`../00_PROJECT/SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md`).

## Note on estimates
The one-week figures are the team's own targets. Earlier duration estimates produced by the agent were rejected as unreliable and removed rather than argued. Treat every duration here as a hypothesis, to be measured against M2, which is the first real data point we will have.
