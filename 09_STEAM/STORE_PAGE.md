# STORE PAGE

_The single most important marketing asset. Built early enough to gather wishlists._

## Must communicate in seconds
- The hook (the one-sentence reason to play and buy).
- The co-op fantasy and the funny / tense moments (via GIFs and short clips).
- Player count and price.

## Elements
Capsules (`CAPSULES.md`), screenshots (`SCREENSHOTS.md`), trailer (`TRAILER.md`), short punchy description, tags, "about" copy.

## Rule
The page must pass the 10-second test: a stranger understands what the game is and why it is fun. Test the pitch independently of the build (risk R9).
