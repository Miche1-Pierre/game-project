# CREATOR STRATEGY

_Streamers and content creators are the main discovery channel for this genre._

## Approach
- Design for creators: legible in 10 seconds, first strong moment comes fast, players stage themselves.
- Identify relevant co-op creators early; offer keys and Steam Playtest access.
- Make it trivial to play with viewers (easy invites, low friction).
- No paid sponsorships before the game proves it produces good moments organically.

## Rule
Creators come because the game is fun to watch and play, not because we paid them. Earn the first wave organically.
