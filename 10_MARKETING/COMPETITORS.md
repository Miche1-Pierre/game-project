# COMPETITORS

_The competitive landscape, from the benchmark. Detail: `../01_RESEARCH/`._

## Direct space (viral co-op / social)
Lethal Company, PEAK, R.E.P.O., Content Warning, Phasmophobia, Among Us, Chained Together, Meccha Chameleon, Big Walk. All Steam, mostly under $10, 1-5 person teams.

## Adjacent (simple core, viral, solo-made)
Buckshot Roulette, Balatro, Megabonk. Different genre, same "small systemic core, huge output" lesson.

## What this means for us
The space is proven and active, but crowded at the "co-op horror extraction" end. We win on **differentiation** (a distinct verb and reason to buy), not on out-producing them. See `USP.md` and `../01_RESEARCH/WHITE_SPACES.md`.
