# WISHLIST STRATEGY

_Positioning and acquisition. Drafted from the Sept. 2026 distribution research (`../01_RESEARCH/VIRALITY_PATTERNS.md`). Tactics only, no concept assumed._

## Principle
We do not buy attention and we cannot. The corpus shows the first wave always comes from third parties reposting a cheap, legible artefact. Our only lever is making that artefact good and removing friction behind it.

## The three things that must ship together
1. **One reveal video** that reads as gameplay footage, not as an advertisement. Jerky camera and real voice chat are assets here, not defects.
2. **The Steam page live at the same minute.** Every hour between "I saw it" and "I can wishlist it" is lost conversion.
3. **A one-sentence claim** that a stranger understands without sound.

## Sequencing (decided 2026-09-16)
- **Before reveal:** nothing public. No teasers, no devlog audience building. We have no audience and cannot build one in time, and the corpus shows we do not need one.
- **Trigger for the reveal:** the tutorial map works end to end in V1. Not before.
- **At reveal:** record video, publish the Steam page and open wishlists, same moment.
- **The month after:** continuous short-form video, community building, and **the next maps keep being built in parallel**. The month is not a pause, which is precisely the failure mode below.
- **Then:** the beta, with the 16-player external protocol (`../11_TESTING/PLAYTESTS.md`).

The go-to-market detail is a separate workstream, opened only once the greybox passes: study what worked and failed for comparable games, then maximise the wishlist.

## Target
The audience is countable, not hypothetical. For the closest comparable, 48% of wishlisters own R.E.P.O., 47% own PEAK and 37% played Meccha Chameleon. We are addressing existing Steam co-op players, not a new market. Full profile in `TARGET_AUDIENCE.md`.

Expected first wave: French-speaking Twitch and YouTube, then European, then international. The audience is trend-driven, which means the launch window matters more than the long tail.

## Forecasting rule
Never state revenue from a wishlist count. Use wishlists times 0.10 to 0.15 for first-week sales, and expect worse above $10. A large wishlist number with a small follower count signals speculative interest, not commitment.

## Failure mode to avoid
Dear Passengers reached 2 M wishlists in 17 days, then went silent for six weeks, missed the gameplay video it promised, and did not ship the demo it announced for Gamescom. A reveal that outruns production converts a spike into decay. **Do not reveal before we can follow up.**

## The wishlist is also the pricing instrument
The team decided the final price is settled by the wishlist curve and the final look, not by preference (`../08_BUSINESS/PRICING.md`). That makes the wishlist a measurement, not just an acquisition target. Record the curve, do not only count the total.
