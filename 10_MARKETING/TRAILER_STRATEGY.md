# TRAILER STRATEGY

_The trailer sells the hook in seconds. Store specs: `../09_STEAM/TRAILER.md`._

## Approach
- Hook in the first 3-5 seconds: the funny or tense co-op moment, no slow intro.
- Real gameplay, punchy cuts, readable.
- One short main trailer + short clips for social.
- In-house capture and edit; paid production only if it clearly lifts conversion (`../08_BUSINESS/COST_MODEL.md`).
