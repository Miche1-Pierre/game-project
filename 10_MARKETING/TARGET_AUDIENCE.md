# TARGET AUDIENCE

_Approach now; specifics after the concept (`../00_PROJECT/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q19)._

## Working profile
- Friend groups of 2 to 4-6 who play co-op together on PC / Steam.
- Players of the corpus (Lethal, PEAK, R.E.P.O., Among Us) and their audiences.
- Streamers and their viewers (the game must be legible to a spectator in 10 seconds).

## To define with the concept
Primary player profile, the games they already play, why they discover ours, why they buy, and what makes them recommend it. Do not define the audience only as "people who like co-op games".
