# TARGET AUDIENCE

_Defined by the team at the 2026-09-16 concept meeting, then cross-checked against benchmark data. See `../01_RESEARCH/VIRALITY_PATTERNS.md`._

## Player profile
Someone coming home from school or work who wants a good evening with friends. Casual, not competitive, not a completionist. They are not looking for a game to master.

This is explicitly **not** a tryhard audience and explicitly **not** a game built to last for years. The team decided the goal is a strong moment, not an evergreen franchise.

## Group size
Four. That is the optimal mode and the design target. Larger groups may become possible on larger maps later, and that is not a first-release concern.

## What they already play
Social chaos co-op with friends. Among Us is the named reference for the "play together and do stupid things" instinct, and the current wave is R.E.P.O., PEAK and their neighbours.

**Measured, not guessed.** For the closest current comparable, 48% of its wishlisters own R.E.P.O., 47% own PEAK and 37% played Meccha Chameleon. Our audience is an existing, countable population on Steam, not a hypothesis.

## Why they discover it
Creators and live streams, above everything else. The team expects a French-speaking Twitch and YouTube first wave, then European, then international.

This matches the benchmark: none of the recent hits bought their audience, and the first wave always came from third parties reposting a legible artefact.

## Why they buy it
Trend effect. They saw it, their friends saw it, and it is cheap. The team is explicit that the purchase driver is the buzz, not a promise of depth.

## Why they recommend it
Same reason. The recommendation is the buzz itself, which means the game has to manufacture the shareable moment on its own (`../01_RESEARCH/WHITE_SPACES.md`).

## What this implies for production
- **The clip has to come from the loop.** If the game does not produce watchable failure without being asked, this audience never hears about it.
- **Price has to be an impulse.** See `../08_BUSINESS/PRICING.md`.
- **Onboarding has to be instant.** A viewer who arrives from a stream must understand the situation in seconds and be able to play within minutes.
- **A trend-driven audience decays.** Plan the launch window deliberately, because there is no long tail to fall back on.

## Open
The creator outreach list, the language priorities beyond French and English, and the regional pricing. Not before the greybox.

## Absorbed from the earlier working profile
The pre-meeting position on `main` described friend groups of 2 to 6 playing co-op on Steam, players of the corpus and their audiences, and streamers plus their viewers. That is consistent with the definition above and adds one requirement worth restating: **the game must be legible to a spectator in 10 seconds**, which is a design constraint, not a marketing one.
