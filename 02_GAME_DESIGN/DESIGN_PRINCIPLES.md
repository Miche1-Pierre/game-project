# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

_The design rules we hold regardless of concept. Derived from VISION and the benchmark. These constrain the concept; they do not choose it._

- **Simple rules, complex situations.** Understand fast; depth comes from interacting systems, not instructions.
- **Systems over content.** One interaction that creates dozens of situations beats dozens of scripted ones.
- **Emergence over scripting.** Define the rules of the world; let players author the moments.
- **Fun before production.** A greybox must show the fun; art amplifies, it does not rescue.
- **Replayability through variation.** Players, procedural, physics and RNG, not grind.
- **Readability.** Understandable to players and spectators; unexpected is not arbitrary.
- **Funny failure over punishment.** Failing is the reward, not the sanction.
- **Social friction, readable in 10 seconds.** The real engine (benchmark thesis).
- **Streamer / clip potential is a property, not the goal.** Fun with nobody recording comes first.
- **One strong verb.** If we cannot name the core verb, the concept is not ready.
