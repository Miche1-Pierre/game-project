# SUCCESS CRITERIA

## Greybox criterion (go / no-go)
Spontaneous laughter or tension after a few tries, AND an immediate urge to replay. Otherwise, back to design (we do not make the 3D models).

## The 8 concept validation tests
1. **Sentence:** explainable in one sentence?
2. **Video:** understood in 10 s, without context?
3. **Group:** immediately funny with friends?
4. **Clip:** does a match produce a shareable moment?
5. **Replayability:** do we want to replay right away?
6. **Emergence:** do unplanned strategies appear?
7. **Differentiation:** can we say why it is not a clone?
8. **Production:** finishable by two, within our budget?

A concept that fails "sentence", "video" or "clip" goes back to prototype.
