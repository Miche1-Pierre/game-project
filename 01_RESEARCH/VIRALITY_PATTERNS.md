# VIRALITY PATTERNS

_Why these games spread. Extracted from the corpus. See also `WHITE_SPACES.md` and `SUCCESS_PATTERNS.md`._

- **The clip generates itself.** A match spontaneously produces a shareable moment (Content Warning makes it literal). Pattern J.
- **Spectator legibility.** A stranger understands the stakes in under 10 seconds. This is the real viral engine.
- **Dramatic irony.** The viewer knows more than the player (Among Us), turning watching into complicity.
- **Memeable failure.** Funny failure (ragdolls, collapses) is inherently clippable and re-shareable.
- **Low price + friend-group buy-in.** ~$10 or less makes "everyone buy it" trivial (Buckshot at $2.99).
- **Creator-first shape.** Easy to explain on stream, the first strong moment comes fast, players stage themselves.
- **Occasional accelerants.** A free launch day (Content Warning gave away 6.2 M copies) floods feeds with footage.

## For us
Design at least one of the top three properties in on purpose, not by accident. The clip and spectator-legibility are the under-exploited ones.
