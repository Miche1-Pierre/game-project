# Lethal Company

_Corpus analysis. Numbers cross-checked, Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2023 - $9.99 - Zeekerss (solo) - 1-4 players - Unity - ~10 M sold - 239 k peak CCU - Overwhelmingly Positive.

## Hook
This game is different because: quota + expedition in the dark + forced separation + sound-based danger.

## Signature verb
**EXTRACT.**

## Fantasy
Broke coworkers scavenging a lethal moon for a heartless corporation.

## Core loop
Land, split up, scavenge, haul loot back, survive/escape, sell, meet the rising quota, repeat harder.

## Central rule
Hit the rising quota or everyone loses.

## Signature mechanic
Proximity voice + walkie in the dark: communication is a resource you manage under risk.

## Social loop
Split up for value, regroup for safety, narrate the danger over voice. Panic is shared.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: watching plans collapse in real time. Tension: quota + darkness + separation + monsters. Replay: procedural moons + player chaos.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Mostly systemic (procedural generation + AI monsters + physics), very little handmade content.

## Differentiator
The quota + expedition + separation loop, with sound as the danger channel.

## What we take for our game
Comms-as-risk and the split/regroup tension are cheap and generate stories. The quota is a clean, legible pressure. A solo dev shipped this: scope can be tiny.
