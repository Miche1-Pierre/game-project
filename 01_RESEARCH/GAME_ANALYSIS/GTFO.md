# GTFO

_Corpus analysis. Numbers cross-checked, Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2021 - ~$35 - 10 Chambers - 1-4 players - Unity - ~1 M sold - modest CCU - Very Positive.

## Hook
This game is different because: hardcore co-op stealth, whisper and coordinate or die.

## Signature verb
**INFILTRATE.**

## Fantasy
An elite team doing terrifying salvage in a monster-infested complex.

## Core loop
Plan, stealth through sleeping monsters, complete the objective, extract under pressure, repeat.

## Central rule
Coordinate precisely and stay quiet; noise triggers overwhelming hordes.

## Signature mechanic
Tense stealth plus mandatory tight coordination, with proximity voice as a tool.

## Social loop
Whispered planning, synchronized action, shared failure.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: the tension and the clean coordinated execution. Tension: stealth + detection + hordes + scarcity. Replay: hard levels + team execution.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Handmade expeditions: heavier content, hardcore niche.

## Differentiator
Hardcore, coordination-mandatory stealth.

## What we take for our game
It shows the ceiling of "danger + communication" taken seriously, and its cost: hard, content-heavy, niche. We want its tension without its difficulty and its content load.
