# Schedule I

_Corpus analysis. Numbers cross-checked, Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2025 - $19.99 - "Tyler" / TVGS (solo) - 1-4 co-op - Unity - 8 M+ sold - 459 k peak CCU - Overwhelmingly Positive.

## Hook
This game is different because: a concrete illegal activity that becomes an empire to automate.

## Signature verb
**RUN A BUSINESS.**

## Fantasy
Build a drug empire from a street corner to a fully automated operation.

## Core loop
Produce, distribute and sell, earn, buy property and employees, automate, scale, repeat.

## Central rule
Grow the operation while avoiding the law.

## Signature mechanic
A deep production, distribution and automation chain: progression grafted onto a concrete activity.

## Social loop
Light co-op division of labor; mostly a systemic sim.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: the empire snowball. Tension: money + police detection + logistics. Replay: progression + emergent logistics.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Heavily systemic (automation), with some handmade world.

## Differentiator
A concrete illegal job turned into an automatable empire.

## What we take for our game
Property 10 (progression/automation on a concrete activity), under-exploited in true social multiplayer. Warning: it is near-solo and the heavy end of scope. Our 2-week budget cannot copy its depth; we would take the "concrete job becomes addictive" idea, not the size.
