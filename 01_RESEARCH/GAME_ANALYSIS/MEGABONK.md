# Megabonk

_Corpus analysis. Added Sept. 2026 on Pierre's suggestion. Cross-checked (`../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2025 (Sept 18) - $9.99 - vedinad (solo) - 1 player - engine unknown - 3D Vampire-Survivors-like roguelike. 100k copies in 3 days, 1M in 2 weeks, 117 k peak CCU, ~$10 M gross. Best Debut Indie nominee, TGA 2025.

## Hook
This game is different because: a 3D take on the auto-battler survivor genre that snowballs into absurd power.

## Signature verb
**SNOWBALL (survive).**

## Fantasy
Start weak, become an unstoppable screen-clearing machine.

## Core loop
Fight waves, collect XP, pick upgrades, build synergies, snowball, die, restart with knowledge.

## Central rule
Survive escalating waves; power scales through stacked upgrades.

## Signature mechanic
Stacking upgrade combinatorics on a simple move-and-survive core (Pattern A/I in 3D action).

## Social loop
Single-player, but "broken build" and "screen explodes" moments are extremely clippable.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: the power-fantasy snowball. Tension: escalating waves + build risk. Replay: RNG + build variety.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Almost entirely systemic (few assets, huge combinatorics).

## Differentiator
Vampire-Survivors depth brought into 3D, solo-made, extremely fast rise.

## What we take for our game
Confirms Balatro's lesson in a different genre: one simple core + stacked combinatorics equals huge depth, cheap to produce, viral clips. A solo dev hit 1M in 2 weeks. Not co-op, but the "small systemic core, big output" model is exactly ours.
