# Content Warning

_Corpus analysis. Numbers cross-checked, Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2024 - $8 - Landfall x Zorbus - 1-4 players - Unity - 2.2 M paid (+6.2 M free on day one) - 205 k peak CCU - Very Positive.

## Hook
This game is different because: the camera is the weapon and the goal, film the danger to get views.

## Signature verb
**FILM.**

## Fantasy
Wannabe content creators risking their lives for a viral clip.

## Core loop
Dive into the Old World, film scary or spectacular moments, extract the footage, upload it, earn views, buy gear, repeat.

## Central rule
Views are the score; you must film danger to earn.

## Signature mechanic
Filming as the objective: the clip is the win condition.

## Social loop
Stage moments for the camera, push each other into danger for better footage.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: performing for the lens. Tension: survive while filming, limited time. Replay: procedural + player performance.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Systemic, and crucially the players generate the content themselves.

## Differentiator
The clip IS the product, literally built into the loop.

## What we take for our game
Pattern J made explicit, and the strongest under-exploited idea in the whole corpus. Folding "make the clip" into the core loop is the frontier we care about most.
