# DEAR PASSENGERS

_Corpus analysis. Added Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`). **Unreleased.** It is in the corpus as a distribution case study, not as a commercial result._

## Identity
Announced July 2026, unreleased as of Sept. 16 2026 - no price announced - FLEXUS (Kyiv, 70+ staff) - player count never stated - **Unity** - 2 M+ wishlists, 3rd most wishlisted game on Steam.

## Hook
This game is different because: one of you flies the plane while the rest fail to run the cabin.

## Signature verb
**SERVE** (under a failing aircraft).

## Fantasy
Working for the worst airline in the world, in the air, with your friends.

## Core loop
One player pilots. The others feed passengers, fight fires, handle turbulence and eject the uncooperative ones. Physics decides how badly it goes.

## Central rule
The plane must keep flying while nobody is qualified to keep it flying.

## Signature mechanic
Physics-driven passengers, luggage and cabin objects in a confined, moving container. The container itself tilts, which is the twist on R.E.P.O.-style object physics.

## Social loop
Forced division of labour with an asymmetric role (pilot vs cabin), in a space small enough that every mistake is seen by everyone.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Unproven. No public build, no demo, no hands-on press. Everything below is about the announcement, not the game.

## Technical profile
- **Engine: Unity**, stated by the studio itself in a Steam announcement that attached a raw Unity Editor screenshot. The studio's own site recruits Unity developers, graphics programmers and technical artists.
- The screenshot shows hand-authored interaction anchors (eat point, item point, enter point, head look bone) drawn by custom editor tooling, plus joint and collider gizmos on every seated passenger, so passengers are PhysX ragdolls.
- **Netcode, player count and voice chat are all unknown.** No source states any of them. The widely repeated "4 players" and "proximity voice chat" figures are press extrapolation from PEAK and R.E.P.O., not statements by the studio.
- The only technical statement from the founder: the biggest problem was the combination of physics and multiplayer, because physics in co-op works badly.
- Unity version and render pipeline are not publicly determinable. URP is a reasonable guess from a 4 GB install and a GTX 1060 minimum, nothing more.

## Production reality
Development started around November or December 2025, targeting roughly 12 months. The studio has shipped four mobile titles totalling 300 M+ downloads and published an engineering article on building their own scripting language for Unity, with an ANTLR parser, a bytecode compiler and a C# virtual machine. This is a mid-size professional studio, not a two-person indie.

## Distribution: the actual case study
The studio did not distribute the trailer. The founder published the Steam page and went to sleep. A third-party indie-news aggregator account posted it, reaching about 47 M views. The chain then ran aggregator, then The Game Awards Instagram, then TikTok, then games press.

| Date 2026 | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Jul 14 | Steam page and trailer go live |

| Jul 17 | 1 M in two days, top 25 most wishlisted |

The studio's own X account has 435 followers. That is the proof the wave was not bought.

## Warning signs
- **Soft interest.** 1 M wishlists against roughly 30 k followers is a ratio of about 1:35, where a committed audience sits near 12:1. Analysts read it as speculative, converting only below $10.
- **Silence.** No Steam announcement since July 31. The gameplay video promised that day never appeared. A public demo was announced to follow Gamescom in late August and has not shipped.
- No date, no price, no demo, no reviews.

## What we take for our game
1. **The trailer is the campaign.** Build the reveal asset so short-form repost accounts mistake it for gameplay clip content, and publish the Steam page at the same moment so there is no friction between seeing it and wishlisting it.
2. **Audience overlap is measurable before you build.** 48% of its wishlisters own R.E.P.O., 47% own PEAK, 37% played Meccha Chameleon. That is our target audience, and it is countable.
3. **Concept is not the moat.** A direct clone called Banana Airways launched the same concept at the same time and collected 11 Steam followers.
4. **Do not announce before you can deliver.** Six weeks of silence after a 2 M wishlist spike is the failure mode where marketing outruns production. This is the single most transferable warning for our own M4 plan.

## Note on sourcing
At least eleven look-alike domains publish invented specifics about this game (player counts, proximity voice, booth numbers). The only official site is `dearpassengers.game`. Treat everything else as an affiliate farm.
