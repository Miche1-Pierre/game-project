# Overcooked 2

_Corpus analysis. Numbers cross-checked, Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2018 - ~$25 - Ghost Town / Team17 - 1-4 players - Unity - several M sold - Very Positive.

## Hook
This game is different because: a readable job role plus a clock, coordination under pressure, hilarious.

## Signature verb
**COOK.**

## Fantasy
A chaotic kitchen brigade barely holding it together.

## Core loop
Read the orders, split roles (prep / cook / plate / wash), serve before the timer, escalate.

## Central rule
Serve orders in time; the kitchen actively fights you.

## Signature mechanic
Legible job roles + time pressure in a hostile, dynamic environment.

## Social loop
Shout instructions, hand off tasks, recover from mistakes together.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: communication-under-pressure comedy. Tension: the clock + moving hazards. Replay: escalating levels + coordination skill.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Handmade levels: heavier content than the systemic games.

## Differentiator
Readable roles + a hostile kitchen + the clock.

## What we take for our game
The "I am the X" role and time pressure (patterns E and F). Caution: it leans on handmade level content our budget cannot afford. We would need the roles and hazards to be systemic, not authored level by level.
