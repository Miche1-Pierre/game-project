# Among Us

_Corpus analysis. Numbers cross-checked, Sept. 2026 (see `../RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`)._

## Identity
2018 (exploded 2020) - $4.99 - InnerSloth (3 people) - 4-15 players - Unity - 500 M+ MAU at peak - 438 k peak CCU - Very Positive.

## Hook
This game is different because: hidden identity + vote, the social debate IS the gameplay.

## Signature verb
**ACCUSE.**

## Fantasy
Be the impostor getting away with it, or the one who catches them.

## Core loop
Do tasks or sabotage, find bodies, call a meeting, debate, vote, repeat.

## Central rule
Crew finish tasks or eject impostors; impostors kill without getting caught.

## Signature mechanic
Hidden role plus a discussion/vote meeting, with dramatic irony for spectators.

## Social loop
Lie, accuse, defend, form and break trust.

## Fun / tension / replayability
Fun: the bluff and the reveal. Tension: hidden danger among players + information asymmetry. Replay: the players are the content.

## Content: systems vs handmade
Almost entirely systemic; players generate everything.

## Differentiator
Hidden identity resolved by a vote meeting.

## What we take for our game
The spectator-knows-more dramatic irony, and the instantly-understood role ("I am the impostor"). Both are under-exploited in a real-time, physical form.
