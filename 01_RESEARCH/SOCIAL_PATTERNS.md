# SOCIAL PATTERNS

_What players do to each other. The social loop is where the stories come from._

## Interaction types seen in the corpus
- **Cooperation** under incomplete information (Lethal, GTFO).
- **Mutual aid and accidental sabotage** via physics (PEAK, R.E.P.O., Chained).
- **Betrayal / hidden agenda** (Among Us).
- **Communication under risk** (proximity chat everywhere).
- **Blame and negotiation** (who dropped it, who is lying).
- **Performing for each other and the camera** (Content Warning).

## What makes a social loop generate stories
- An information gap that forces talking.
- Interdependence: my action affects you.
- A reason to split up AND a reason to regroup.
- Legible roles, so intentions read instantly.

## For us
The social loop, not the setting, is the product. Pick interactions that manufacture friction between players, and make that friction readable.
