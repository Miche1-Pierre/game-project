# REPLAYABILITY PATTERNS

_Where replayability comes from in the corpus, and what a two-person team can afford._

## Sources seen
- **Players** (the other humans are the variation): cheapest, strongest. Among Us, Gang Beasts.
- **Procedural generation** (moons, mountains, layouts): Lethal, PEAK, R.E.P.O.
- **Physics** (never the same twice): PEAK, R.E.P.O., Chained.
- **RNG + combinatorics** (build variety): Balatro, Buckshot.
- **Roles and hidden information** (each round reshuffles): Among Us.
- **Progression** (Schedule I): heavier, content-hungry.
- **Handmade levels** (Overcooked, GTFO): the expensive kind.

## For us (from CONSTRAINTS)
Prefer replayability from **players + procedural + physics + RNG**, which are systemic and cheap. Avoid depending on handmade levels or grind. Replayability must be tested in greybox (risks R10 and R13).
