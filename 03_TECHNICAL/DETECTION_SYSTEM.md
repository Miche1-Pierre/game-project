## Principles (from the benchmark)
- Prefer **player-driven detection** (witnesses, suspicion, accusation) over expensive NPC-AI detection. Cheaper and more emergent (H3).
- If NPC detection is needed, keep it legible: clear vision cones, clear noise radius, clear states, so players and spectators understand why they were caught.
- "Unexpected does not mean arbitrary" (VISION): detection must read.
