# Setup

Unity 6000.6.1f1, forced to Direct3D11 (D3D12 crashes the editor on this hybrid
NVIDIA plus Intel laptop). Scene `Assets/_Movers/Scenes/Tutorial_01.unity`.
Solo, in the editor, no networking.

Controls: WASD or ZQSD, mouse to look, left click grab and drop, right click
throw, E to deliver when every required object is loaded, Escape frees the cursor.
