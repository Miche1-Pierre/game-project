# SOUND DESIGN

_What makes a sound. Feedback for gameplay events._

## Approach
- Map each important event to a sound through the audio service (`../03_TECHNICAL/AUDIO_SYSTEM.md`).
- Categories we will need: interactions (grab, use, break), impacts, alerts, success / failure, UI.
- Spatial 3D for world sounds (helps readability of where things happen).
- Layer simple free samples rather than commissioning custom sound design.

_Concrete event-to-sound list follows the concept._
